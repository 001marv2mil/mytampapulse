### ⭐ Community Pick: Taylor Dillon + Kayla Chang of Con Amor

Taylor Dillon and Kayla Chang started making masa from their apartment. Not a restaurant, not a pop-up, their apartment, with a grinder and a process that takes two days before anyone even eats. That's nixtamalization: soaking raw corn, processing it by hand, grinding it fresh. Most places skip this entirely and open a bag. Taylor and Kayla built a whole restaurant around refusing to do that.

They opened Con Amor at the base of the Avenue Lofts on Florida Ave in Seminole Heights. People who've found it have started texting their friends about it. That's the whole marketing strategy, and honestly it's working. When the food is that specific, word moves on its own.

Con Amor is at 5240 N Florida Ave, Tampa. Small spot, tight menu, fresh tortillas every day.

*The kind of people who make you realize how much shortcuts cost.*
