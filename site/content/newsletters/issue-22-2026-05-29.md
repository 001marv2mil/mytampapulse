### 🎯 Marv's Tampa Pro Tips

- **Teen Suicide, Cloud Nothings, and Fanclub Wallet at Crowbar** Wednesday May 27, 7:00 PM, $29. Crowbar is the Ybor rock bar that's been holding the line since 2008. Small room, big sound, gets sweaty fast. Cloud Nothings is Cleveland indie rock royalty, Teen Suicide is the kind of band where the name does a lot of work, and Fanclub Wallet is the one you'll be telling people you saw "back when." Doors at 7, opener probably hits at 7:45. Park on 7th and walk.
- **NITE at New World Music Hall** Thursday May 28, 7:00 PM, $20. New World is the old church on Howard Avenue that got reborn as a venue. The bones make the sound and the room feels like nowhere else in Tampa. NITE leans electronic and atmospheric, fits the space.
- **The Notebook (Touring) at the Straz Carol Morsani Hall** Thursday and Friday, 7:30 PM. Two nights only and then it's gone. The Morsani is the Straz's big house, 2,600 seats, parking under Curtis Hixon makes the walk easy. Grab dinner at Anise on Whiting first, you'll have time.
- **Grizz CLL and Vick Vapors at New World Music Hall** Friday May 29, 7:00 PM, $20. Underground Florida hip hop, Grizz is local Tampa, room's going to be hot. Cheapest worthwhile ticket on the schedule.
- **Kid Rock's Freedom 250 Tour at MIDFLORIDA** Saturday May 30, 6:30 PM. The amphitheater sits out at the State Fairgrounds. Get out there early, traffic on I-4 east of downtown will be a situation. Tailgating culture is real in those lots. Bring cash for parking.
- **Shinedown's Dance, Kid, Dance Act II at Benchmark** Saturday May 30, 7:00 PM. Indoor arena downtown, totally different vibe than Kid Rock the same night. If you want air conditioning and a roof, this is your pick.
- **The Black Crowes and Whiskey Myers on the Southern Hospitality Tour at MIDFLORIDA** Sunday May 31, 6:30 PM. Joint bill. Southern rock meets red dirt country, the right pairing on paper, and the Crowes are touring behind the best material they've cut in a decade.

### 📋 Digest

- 🔥 **Two Shepherds Taproom is closing its current run, reborn as a board game and TCG shop with event space**. Seminole Heights' beer scene shifts again. Honestly, this is a smart pivot for a smaller pour room. The community was always the draw; now it's the whole product.
- 🍽️ **The best beach bars to visit right now, from St. Pete Beach to Clearwater Beach**. Time of year when the Gulf side starts living up to the postcards. Pull a Friday early and make the run.
- 🎵 **Tampa International Fringe Fest takes Ybor City**. Tampa's wildest arts week. Theater, weird experimental stuff, ten venues across the strip. Cheap tickets, big swings.
- 🌉 **Cross bay pedestrian path and free express lane now open on the new Howard Frankland Bridge**. You can finally walk between Tampa and St. Pete, which is wild to say out loud. Sunrise crossing club starts now.
- 🌟 **New Marriott set for June opening in iconic St. Pete Beach location**. The long promised redevelopment of that corner of the sand finally has a date. Worth a peek if you've been watching the project.
- ⛵ **Red Bull Cliff Diving World Series St. Pete coming back to the Bay**. World class athletes throwing themselves off platforms into the harbor. Free to watch from the pier. One of the most underrated free shows on the calendar.
- 🏖️ **Summer refresh: our favorite spots for local home decor in South Tampa**. Hyde Park Village adjacent, when you need to redo a room without ordering everything from Amazon.

---

### 💎 Hidden Gems

- 🗝️ **Dunedin downtown weekend**. Easiest day trip from Tampa. Walkable downtown, three breweries within blocks, the Pinellas Trail running right through. Dog friendly everywhere. Park once, walk for hours.
- 🛍️ **Channelside public skate session at AMALIE Arena**. The Tampa Bay Lightning's practice rink opens for public skate most afternoons. Yes, ice in Tampa. Skates rent cheap. Weirdly fun, especially with a kid.
- 🍦 **Things to do with kids this weekend**. The Florida Aquarium's summer programming is kicking in, Glazer Children's Museum is right downtown, and the Curtis Hixon splash pads beat any backyard pool on an 85 degree afternoon.
- 🎨 **Florida Museum of Photographic Arts, Cube building downtown**. Free to walk in. Underrated, quiet, the kind of place you mention and people say "I had no idea that was here." Twenty minutes to reset the week.
- 🌅 **Davis Islands beach loop**. Pocket beach, dog friendly seawall, downtown skyline view straight across the channel. Five minute drive from downtown, feels like a different city.

### ⭐ Community Pick

> This week the spotlight goes to **the MacDill Honor Guard, the Bay Pines National Cemetery volunteers, and every Tampa Bay family that lost someone in service.** While most of us were on the beach Saturday, hundreds of volunteers were placing flags on every single grave at Bay Pines before sunrise. Eighty thousand graves. Before dawn. No press release, no Instagram story. Just hands and flags and quiet.
>
> Tampa is a military town in ways the rest of Florida isn't. MacDill, the VA hospitals, the families who came for one tour and stayed for thirty years. Memorial Day weekend is the loud version of acknowledging that. The other 51 weeks are the quiet version.
>
> *If you see someone in uniform out grabbing coffee this week, the move is simple. Pay for their drink. Don't make a thing of it.*
