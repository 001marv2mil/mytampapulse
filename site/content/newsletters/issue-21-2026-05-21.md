### ⭐ Community Pick: Phil Alessi Jr. of Alessi Bakery

Phil Alessi Jr. didn't choose the bakery life, it chose him. His family has been baking Cuban bread in Tampa since 1912, more than a hundred years of early mornings, flour-dusted aprons, and the kind of institutional knowledge you can't learn anywhere else. He grew up in the bakery, stepped away for a while, and came back because some things just pull you home. Now he's leading Alessi into a new chapter and somehow making it feel like both a tribute and something totally fresh.

People in Tampa have strong feelings about Alessi's Cuban bread. Like, stronger than you'd expect for bread. I've heard people say they won't eat a Cuban sandwich anywhere that doesn't use Alessi's loaf. That's not marketing. That's just what happens when a family does something right for long enough that it becomes part of the city's identity.

The bakery is one of Tampa's oldest operating businesses, rooted in West Tampa and now writing its next chapter.

*The kind of person who makes Tampa feel like Tampa.*
