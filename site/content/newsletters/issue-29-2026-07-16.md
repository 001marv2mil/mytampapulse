### 🌤️ Tampa's 7-Day Weather Whisper

Peak summer holds with highs in the low 90s most of the week and the usual afternoon storm pattern. Sunday, the day of the World Cup final, actually looks like the mildest day of the week with a high around 88 and a lower storm chance, which is good news for the outdoor watch parties. The rest of the week runs the standard July script. Mornings start clear, storms build through the early afternoon, and things clear out by evening. Plan outdoor time before 1 PM or after 7 PM. The heat index sits above 100 in the afternoons, so hydrate at the festivals and find shade between food stalls.

### 🎯 Marv's Tampa Pro Tips

- **World Cup Final Watch Party**, Al Lang Stadium, 230 1st St SE, St. Pete. Sunday July 19, free, kickoff at 3 PM. The Rowdies have hosted watch parties all tournament and the final is the big one. Big screens, five dollar beers, giveaways before the match, and the St. Pete waterfront behind you. If you have watched any of this World Cup, this is where you want to be for the last game of it. Armature Works in Tampa runs the final from 3 to 5 PM as the indoor alternative.
- **Asian Summer Food and Music Festival**, Curtis Hixon Waterfront Park, 600 N Ashley Dr, Tampa. Friday July 17 from 5 PM, Saturday July 18 from noon, Sunday July 19 from 11 AM. Mega Night Market brings more than 100 vendors of Asian street food, live music, and cultural performances to the Riverwalk for three straight days. This is the same crew that has been packing night markets across the Bay all year. Go hungry, bring cash, and get there early on Saturday before the dinner crowd.
- **Megan Moroney, Cloud 9 Tour**, Benchmark International Arena, 401 Channelside Dr, Tampa. Friday July 17 at 7 PM. One of the biggest names in country music right now on her 2026 tour, and a dollar from every ticket goes to her foundation for anti bullying and mental health work. If country is your lane, this is the show of the week.
- **SharkCon 12**, Florida State Fairgrounds Expo Hall, 4800 US-301, Tampa. Saturday and Sunday July 18 and 19, 9 AM to 5 PM. The largest shark convention in the country, with speakers from Shark Week, National Geographic's SharkFest, and Animal Planet, plus over 150 vendors and a dozen ocean conservation nonprofits. Adult tickets are $24.95, kids $9.95. Genuinely one of the more unusual things Tampa hosts and the kids love it.
- **Lumbre**, 220 N Rome Ave, Tampa. Just opened this week in the NoHo district from the team behind Michelin starred Rocca and Bar Terroir. Spanish cooking built around a wood fire, with traditional paella, seafood tapas, and imported hams and cheeses. Open Sunday through Thursday 5 to 10 PM, Friday and Saturday 5 to 11 PM. The Rocca team does not miss and this room will be hard to book in a month. Go now while a weeknight table is still possible.

### 📋 Digest

- 🏟️ **The Rays dropped the first interior renderings of their proposed new stadium last week and it is starting to look like a real building**. Translucent roof, club and group spaces, and an updated cownose ray tank, with the team targeting a 2029 opening on the Dale Mabry site. Public financing is still being negotiated, but this is the closest the franchise has come to showing what the future actually looks like.
- 🌮 **Rreal Tacos is turning the old Red Mesa Cantina building in downtown St. Pete into a three story taco complex**. The 16,000 square foot space at 128 3rd St S will hold three concepts, five bars, and seating for over 800. St. Pete dining keeps going vertical and this is the most ambitious food project announced in the Bay this year.
- 🍽️ **Baba closed in St. Pete's Grand Central District and a new concept called Kaixo is taking the space**. It comes from chef Andrew Duncan and sommelier Danielle McCoy and is built around the food and wine of the Iberian Peninsula, with the Barra Barra pintxo bar becoming a full time fixture next door. A real loss and a real gain on the same block.
- 🕹️ **GameTime is closing its Ybor City arcade on August 9 after its lease ran out**. The owner pointed to the economy and the direction of Ybor. If you have a reason to go, this is your window before it is gone.
- 🏈 **Bucs camp is about to open with two big contracts hanging over it**. Baker Mayfield and Vita Vea are both looking for new deals, and the offense has a new coordinator in Josh Grizzard after a down 2025. The next few weeks set the tone for the season.
- 🏙️ **Downtown St. Pete keeps stacking towers, with the Art House residential building now open and a massive Gas Plant District plan proposing more than 3,600 affordable housing units**. Love it or hate it, the St. Pete skyline in 2026 does not look like it did five years ago.

### 💎 Hidden Gems

- 🍽️ **Rooster & The Till**, 6500 N Florida Ave, Seminole Heights. A James Beard nominated modern American kitchen sitting in a small storefront on North Florida Avenue that most of Tampa drives past. The menu changes with the season, the room is tiny, and the cooking is as serious as anything downtown at half the noise. Reservations are smart because the space is small. This is the kind of place that quietly defines how good Seminole Heights food has gotten.
- 🥩 **Sa Ri One Grill**, 7525 W Hillsborough Ave, Tampa. All you can eat Korean barbecue where you cook your own dinner on a grill built into the table. It sits in a strip mall on West Hillsborough that gives no signal of how good it is inside. Go with a group, order the marinated short rib and pork belly, and plan to be there a while. One of the best value meals in the city if you come hungry.
- 🍲 **Tindahang Pinoy**, 9150 49th St N, Pinellas Park. Filipino home cooking tucked inside a grocery store, which is exactly why almost nobody outside the community has found it. Steam table plates of adobo, lechon, and pancit, priced like a neighborhood spot because that is what it is. Walk past the aisles to the back, order a combo plate, and eat some of the most honest food in Pinellas County.
- 🌊 **Fort De Soto Park**, 3500 Pinellas Bayway S, Tierra Verde. Regularly ranked among the best beaches in the country and yet the North Beach lagoon stays uncrowded on a weekday morning. 1,100 acres across five keys with a historic fort, seven miles of waterfront, kayak trails, and a fishing pier. Open daily 7 AM to sunset. Go early, bring everything you need because it is a drive from the parking to the good spots, and you will have Gulf shoreline nearly to yourself.
- 🌲 **Hillsborough River State Park**, 15402 US-301 N, Thonotosassa. One of the only places in Florida with actual river rapids, thirty minutes north of downtown Tampa. The Hillsborough River runs over limestone outcrops here, which almost nowhere else in the state does. Hiking trails along the river, a swimming pool fed by the park, and a suspension bridge over the rapids. Entry is a few dollars per car. On a summer weekday morning the trails are quiet and the river is moving.
