### 🎯 Marv's Tampa Pro Tips

- **Hank Williams Jr. at MIDFLORIDA Credit Union Amphitheatre** Friday June 5, 6:30 PM. Bocephus. Outdoor stage. June in Tampa. The show is 6:30 so get there by 5 if you want a spot on the lawn. Country crowd, summer heat, and a setlist built for people who know every word. This is the kind of show that feels like it should have happened in 1994 and is somehow better now.
- **Emo Night Brooklyn at Orpheum Theatre Tampa** Friday June 5, 8:00 PM. The Orpheum on Nebraska is one of the better midsize rooms in the city. All the songs you screamed in a parking lot at 17, played loud in a room full of adults who remember every word. Shows like this sell out quietly. Get there early.
- **Maddix at The Ritz Ybor** Friday June 5, 10:00 PM, 18 and over. Dutch producer, big room electronic, The Ritz is the right venue for it. If you're staying in Ybor after dinner this is your Friday night option.
- **Red Bull Cliff Diving World Series St. Pete** Saturday and Sunday. World class divers launching off platforms into the St. Pete harbor in front of a crowd that spills all the way down the waterfront. This is one of the most visually ridiculous free events Tampa Bay hosts all year. No ticket, no reservation. Just show up, find a spot on the pier, and watch someone throw a perfect back 4.5 somersault from 90 feet up. Bring sunscreen.
- **England vs New Zealand at Raymond James Stadium** Saturday June 6, 4:00 PM. Bring cash for parking, take the trolley if you can. English and Kiwi supporters are both going to be in Tampa for this. The pregame energy around the stadium will be a whole scene before anyone kicks a ball.
- **Tampa Tarpons vs Palm Beach Cardinals homestand** at George Steinbrenner Field, games Thursday through Sunday. Minor league baseball in June at Steinbrenner is genuinely one of Tampa's most underrated experiences. Good sight lines, cheap tickets, cold beer, and you're watching players who could be in the majors in two years. Thursday night games have the best vibe.
- **Global Running Day free 5K on the Riverwalk** Saturday June 7, morning. Curtis Hixon to Amalie and back, organized group run, completely free. Best way to earn the MIDFLORIDA parking lot energy from the night before.

### 📋 Digest

- 🔥 **Colony Grill's arugula salad pizza is the meal of the summer**. New Haven style thin crust, topped post bake with a dressed arugula salad. Sounds like it shouldn't work. Works completely. Hyde Park Village location is the move.
- 🍽️ **Juneteenth gets a creative twist at the Tampa Edition's Arts Club**. The Arts Club on June 19 is doing a full Juneteenth dinner with local artists, live music, and a curated menu. Worth booking early.
- 🎵 **Tampa Bay sports bar named one of the best in America**. Buya in Seminole Heights made the national list. Part izakaya, part sports bar, all Tampa weird in the best way. If you haven't been, this is your nudge.
- 🌟 **Bolts Brew Fest returns this July, one of Tampa's best summer festivals**. Early bird tickets are out. Lightning alumni, local breweries, Amalie Arena. Tampa summer festival season is stacked.
- 🎨 **New rooftop bar opening in Channelside this month**. The waterfront rooftop scene keeps expanding. Another reason the cruise ship crowd is starting to actually stay a few nights before their sail date.
- 🌉 **Howard Frankland Bridge toll free express lane is already changing how people cross the Bay**. The commute conversation downtown is different now. Worth knowing if you're making regular St. Pete runs this summer.
- 🏃 **Global Running Day free 5K on the Riverwalk Saturday morning**. Zero excuses. It's free, it's the Riverwalk, and it's done before the heat fully kicks in.

### 💎 Hidden Gems

- 🗝️ **Safety Harbor pier at sunset**. Quiet little waterfront town 25 minutes from downtown Tampa. The pier, then Bar Fly for cocktails, then back across the causeway as the sky goes orange. One of the most underused day trips in the Bay.
- 🛍️ **Brew Bus Tampa coffee in Ybor**. Tucked into the brewery space off 7th. Old Tampa bones, good espresso, nobody standing in line. Quietest morning spot in Ybor by a wide margin.
- 🌊 **Fort De Soto Beach before 9 AM**. This time of year the beach is perfect before the heat sets in. Flat water, white sand, almost no one there before 9. Park at the north beach lot, walk south. Bring coffee and nowhere to be.
- 🎨 **Tempus Projects in Ybor City at 1624 E 7th Ave**. Free walk in, contemporary art space. Legitimately one of the coolest small galleries in the city and almost no one outside the arts community knows it exists. Twenty minutes will reset your week.
- 🍕 **On the Brix in Ybor City**. Pizza and craft beer in a space that actually has character. Ybor pizza that isn't a tourist trap. Late night hours, outdoor seating, reasonable prices.

### ⭐ Community Pick

> This week the spotlight goes to **the organizers who brought the Red Bull Cliff Diving World Series back to St. Pete for the second consecutive year**. This is not a small lift. Getting a World Series stop in a mid-sized American market requires a pitch to Red Bull's international events team, city permits across two waterfront municipalities, platform construction in an active harbor, safety coordination with the Coast Guard, and crowd management for a free event with no ticket gate to control flow. None of that is easy.
>
> St. Pete's waterfront is one of the best natural event venues in the southeast, and whoever made the case that it belongs on the same calendar as Lisbon and Copenhagen and Sydney did Tampa Bay a real favor. The fact that anyone can walk down to the pier and watch world class athletes fly through the air for free is the kind of thing this city should be proud of.
>
> *Wave at the production crew this weekend. They were here before the athletes arrived and they will be here long after the crowd leaves.*
