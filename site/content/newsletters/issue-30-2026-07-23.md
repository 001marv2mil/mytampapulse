### 🌤️ Tampa's 7-Day Weather Whisper

Peak summer at its hottest this week. Highs sit in the mid 90s every day and the heat index climbs toward 106 in the afternoons, some of the most dangerous heat of the year. The pattern holds. Mornings start clear, storms build by early afternoon, and things clear by evening. Do anything outdoors before 1 PM or after 7 PM, and treat the middle of the day as indoor time. Drink more water than you think you need, especially at the Pier on Sunday and the ballpark all weekend. The overnight lows barely dip below 79, so even the evenings stay warm.

### 🌊 On the Water

- 🌡️ **Bay and Gulf water is running near or above 90 degrees**, four to five degrees warmer than a normal late July. Picnic Island read 91.9 and Clearwater Beach 87.8 this week. It is bath water, easy to swim in and hard on the coral.
- 🟢 **Red tide is clear right now.** The state wildlife commission reported no bloom and no respiratory irritation across the region this week. The next status update comes Friday July 24. For a live beach conditions recording, call 866.300.9399 before you drive out.
- ☀️ **The heat index tops out around 106 in the afternoons.** On the water the sun reflection doubles your burn, so reapply sunscreen every two hours and get off the sandbar before the 2 PM storm cell builds. Afternoon lightning over the bay is the real summer danger, not the heat.

### 💎 Hidden Gems

- 🎣 **Skyway Fishing Pier State Park**, Interstate 275 at the Sunshine Skyway, St. Pete side and Terra Ceia side. The approaches to the old Sunshine Skyway bridge were turned into what is billed as the world's longest fishing pier, and you drive your car right out onto it. Open 24 hours, small entry fee per vehicle. Lit at night, bait shops on site, and pelicans working the rails. Sunrise from the middle of the bay with a coffee is one of the more underrated mornings in Pinellas.
- 🏝️ **Egmont Key**, off the mouth of Tampa Bay. An island state park you can only reach by boat, with a historic fort, an 1858 lighthouse, and some of the clearest snorkeling water in the area. Ferries run from Fort De Soto. Bring everything you need because there is nothing to buy out there, and go on a calm morning. It feels a hundred miles from the city and it is fifteen.
- 🌳 **Philippe Park**, 2525 Philippe Pkwy, Safety Harbor. The oldest park in Pinellas County, sitting on Old Tampa Bay under enormous live oaks, with a Tocobaga Indian temple mound you can walk up. Free, shaded, and rarely crowded on a weekday. Bring a picnic, take the shade, and look out at the same water the Tocobaga did five hundred years ago.
- 🌺 **Sunken Gardens**, 1825 4th St N, St. Pete. A tropical garden more than a hundred years old tucked into a city block on 4th Street, with flamingos, koi, and paths under a dense canopy. Small admission. It is the best rainy afternoon move in St. Pete when the storms roll in, because the canopy keeps you covered and the crowds stay away in summer.
- 🍨 **Bo's Ice Cream**, 7101 N Florida Ave, Seminole Heights. A Tampa institution since 1955, still scooping in the same walk up spot on Florida Avenue. No app, no line most weeknights, just soft serve and sundaes the way they have made them for seventy years. If the ice cream festival on Sunday gets you in the mood, this is the low key version any night of the week.
